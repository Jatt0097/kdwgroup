About Kitchen Dekor Werks

Combining architectural discipline, millwork expertise, and construction project management.

OUR FOUNDING PRINCIPLES

Bridging Architectural Design & Precision Manufacturing

Kitchen Dekor Werks was born out of a passion for refined craftsmanship, architectural precision, and uncompromising quality. Founded with a deep-rooted background in architecture, kitchen design, and high-end residential millwork management across Ontario, our team bridges the gap between sophisticated design and flawless field execution.

From complex custom kitchen installations and architectural wall paneling to specialized built-in cabinetry, we approach every project with an eye for detail. We understand that true luxury lies in the execution—ensuring every joint, finish, and mechanism is meticulously engineered to stand the test of time.

Custom Engineering

Every pantry unit and door profile is CAD-modeled to integrate seamlessly with existing millwork and structural dimensions.

Factory Spray Finishes

Industrial-grade lacquer applications ensuring smooth touch, stain resistance, and precise color-matching for existing islands and cabinets.

Our Production & Installation Standards

How we guarantee consistency across every residential and commercial installation.

Architectural Accuracy

Every fabrication begins with comprehensive shop drawings, confirming clearances, electrical locations, and scribing allowances before production starts.

Hardware & Material Selection

Turnkey Site Execution

Our team manages the full lifecycle—from measurement to shop spraying, white-glove site delivery, scribe-fitting, and hardware adjustment.
