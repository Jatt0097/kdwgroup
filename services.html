What we build.

Full kitchen design and fabrication built around your space, your storage needs, and how you actually cook. From layout and material selection through door style, hardware, and finish, every cabinet is built to order in our shop.

  • Face-frame & frameless construction
  • Custom sizing for awkward or non-standard spaces
  • Islands, pantries, and appliance integration

Mouldings, wainscoting, coffered ceilings, and trim, milled to match existing profiles or drawn from scratch. Finished and installed on site to a tight tolerance.

  • Crown, base, and casing profiles
  • Wainscoting & wall panelling
  • Custom mantels and architectural details

Window seats, media walls, home offices, and closets built into the room rather than dropped into it. Designed to use every inch of an awkward space.

  • Media walls & entertainment units
  • Closets, mudrooms & pantries
  • Window seats & reading nooks

Full-service kitchen renovation from concept to final walkthrough, including demo coordination, electrical and plumbing rough-in liaison, and final install by our own crew.

  • Project management start to finish
  • Coordination with trades
  • In-house installation team
